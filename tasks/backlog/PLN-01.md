# PLN-01: Candidate Ambient Screensaver Shaders (Pixel Rain, Random Lasers, Rotating Tetriminos)

## 1. Candidate Concepts
1. **Normal Pixel Rain**: Simple, elegant, minimalist retro vertical square/rect pixel drops without Japanese/Latin glyph overhead.
2. **Random Lasers**: High-energy geometric horizontal/vertical scanning beams cutting across viewport dead space to actively exercise LCD subpixels.
3. **Rotating Tetriminos**: Classic polyomino shapes gently floating, rotating, and drifting through space in soft accent colors.

## 2. Status
Roadmap candidate for user prioritization.
